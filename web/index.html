    Add photos of the label

    For the best result, add the ingredient list and the nutrition table. Hold the phone flat and fill the frame with the text.

    Up to 4 photos. You can check and correct what we read before the report.

    Type or paste the label

    Copy the ingredient list and nutrition table from the pack or a shopping site. Include the line that starts with “Ingredients”.

    Check what we read

    Compare these with the pack and fix anything that’s wrong. Leave a box empty if it isn’t on the label.

    Point at the barcode

    Hold steady about 15 cm away.
